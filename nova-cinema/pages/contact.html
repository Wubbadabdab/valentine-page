Frequently asked questions

Can I bring my own snacks?

We'd love you to try the galley, but small outside snacks are fine. Please leave full meals — and any Sea King meat — at home.

Is cosplay allowed?

Absolutely! Costumes are encouraged at every One Piece screening. Props must be soft or foam, and big hats come off once the lights go down so everyone can see.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes — up to 2 hours before showtime. Cancel from My tickets on the schedule page, or call the crew on (555) 012-3456.

How do group bookings work?

You can book up to 10 seats online, and booking 10 together unlocks our 15% crew discount. For groups of up to 30, use the form above and we'll reserve a block of seats for you.

Is Nova Cinema accessible?

Every hall has step-free access, wheelchair spaces, companion seats and assisted-listening headsets. Let us know in your message if you need anything else.

Are the films kid-friendly?

One Piece films are action-packed adventures with some intense battles. Younger Nakama are welcome with an adult — Adventures in Alabasta and Strong World are family favorites.
