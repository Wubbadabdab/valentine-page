          Travel tips

          Small habits, better trips

          A few lessons that make almost any journey smoother.

          1. 01

            Travel in shoulder season

            Late spring and early autumn often bring better prices, softer light and fewer crowds.

          2. 02

            Book the hard-to-get things first

            Permits, popular tours and special stays can sell out — lock them in before the rest of your plans.

          3. 03

            Pack light, layer smart

            A few versatile layers handle changing weather and make moving between places easier.

          4. 04

            Learn a few local phrases

            Hello, please and thank you go a long way and open the door to warmer conversations.

          5. 05

            Respect local customs

            Dress modestly at religious sites, ask before photographing people and follow local etiquette.

          6. 06

            Sort out travel insurance

            It’s the least exciting part of planning — and the one you’ll be most glad you did.
